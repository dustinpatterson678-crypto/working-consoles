## Summary (one line)
Working game consoles, arcade cabinets and a full redemption arcade you can play inside your world.

## Description
**Dustin's Arcade** brings a real arcade into your world.

- **16 ticket redemption games** with realistic machine models: Alley Roller, Hoop Frenzy, Mole Bonk, Coin Cascade, Prize Claw, Tower Stack, Light Chaser, Swamp Smash, Big Wheel, Color Echo, Bonus Drum, Clown Knockdown, Plinko Drop, High Striker, Air Hockey and Lucky Locker.
- **Five pinball tables**, each with its own playfield: Classic, Galaxy Run, Haunted Hollow, Pirate's Gold and Turbo Speedway.
- **Prize counter and prize wall**: trade your tickets for real items, from plushies to working consoles. Server owners can edit the prize list in `config/arcadeplus_prizes.txt`.
- **One-block arcade building**: place it and a whole arcade builds itself, with every machine placed and plugged in.
- **Working consoles and computers** that play on in-world TVs, from 8-bit to disc era, plus streaming TVs and projectors.

### Requirements
Fabric Loader 0.19.5+, Fabric API, GeckoLib 5.5.7. Java 25. Mod Menu optional.

### Important
This pack ships **no BIOS files, no games and no ROMs**. Consoles only play dumps you make yourself from hardware and media you own. Files that look downloaded from the internet are refused.
